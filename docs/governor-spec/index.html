Governor Contract Spec

The governor is the only actor in a turn that holds a decision. It sets the contract at admission, schedules every round against it, and decides when the turn ends — including ending it early.

Spec owner chat seat (orchestrator) Build owner governor seat Companion Orchestrator Turn Schema Date 2026-09-10
01

Authority

Every other actor in the loop executes. Tool selection proposes, the model answers, tools run, results are classified. None of them decides whether the turn continues. That is the governor's alone, and it is the property that makes the loop auditable — one decider means one place to ask why did this turn do that.

Close the open gaps — within the prescribed time, within cost, at the desired quality, without errors.
TimeThe promise clock, from POST to publish
CostLLM spend, the dominant term today
QualityThe tier's posture: none · some · best
ErrorsTool failures and dead ends, not silently absorbed

Everyone contributes to this contract; only the governor sets it. That asymmetry is the spec.

04

The interface

Three objects. The contract is set once; the directive is produced every round; the disposition is produced once, at exit.

TurnContract            # set ONCE at admission, never re-made mid-turn
  promise_version : str        # the promise this turn is judged against
  tier            : str        # fast | normal | thinking | null
  budget_time_s   : float|None # null when the tier promised nothing
  budget_cost_c   : float|None
  quality_bar     : str        # none | some | best
  unpromised_why  : str|None   # populated when the terms are null

RoundDirective           # produced at the TOP of every round
  action          : continue | finalize | abort
  reason          : str        # human-readable, and recorded
  gaps_targeted   : [GapId]    # which gaps this round is for
  budget          : {tool_calls:int, time_slice_s:float, cost_slice_c:float}
  agent_role      : str
  reasoning_depth : str
  run_critic      : bool

TurnDisposition          # produced ONCE, at exit
  outcome         : completed | finalized_early | aborted
  use_enrichers   : bool       # integrate quality-for-cost call
  terms_met       : {time:bool|None, cost:bool|None, quality:bool|None, errors:bool}
  unmet_reason    : str|None

Why terms_met is tri-state. A term the turn could not measure must read null, never false and never true. Could-not-check and checked-and-failed are different facts, and collapsing them is the defect this program has catalogued more than any other.

05

Rules the governor must hold

  1. Do not own the gap set.

    The governor reads gaps; it must not write them. An actor that both produces and consumes its own evidence cannot be checked — and "the loop terminated because the governor said its own gaps were closed" is unfalsifiable. One writer, many readers.

  2. Never invent a term.

    A turn whose tier promised nothing carries null budgets and an unpromised_why. Defaulting them to a neighbouring tier's numbers manufactures a contract nobody offered.

  3. Every directive records its inputs.

    "Extend" cannot be checked against anything. "extend · gaps 2 · 41s left of 95 · 18¢ of 45¢ · quality unmeasured" can. Every input that could have changed the branch travels with the branch.

  4. Be the only granter of continuation.

    Two actors granting extensions from one ledger, on two different deadline defaults, is a defect that exists today. Sole authority retires it rather than repairing it.

  5. Declare a floor as a floor.

    While react_1 under-reports cost, the cost term is a lower bound. A governor that spends against an understated number and reports the budget kept is wrong in the direction that costs money.
